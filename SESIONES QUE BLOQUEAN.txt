Identificar sesiones bloqueadas y bloqueadoras:
sql

SELECT
    s1.username || '@' || s1.machine || ' (SID=' || s1.sid || ')' AS blocker,
    s2.username || '@' || s2.machine || ' (SID=' || s2.sid || ')' AS waiter,
    s1.sql_id AS blocker_sql_id,
    s2.sql_id AS waiter_sql_id,
    s2.seconds_in_wait,
    s2.event
FROM v$session s1
JOIN v$session s2 ON s1.sid = s2.blocking_session
WHERE s2.blocking_session IS NOT NULL;


Esta consulta muestra quién está bloqueando a quién, el SID de cada uno y la sentencia SQL involucrada.
Ver objetos bloqueados:


sql
SELECT lo.session_id, lo.oracle_username, lo.os_user_name,
       ao.object_name, ao.object_type, lo.locked_mode
FROM v$locked_object lo
JOIN dba_objects ao ON lo.object_id = ao.object_id;
